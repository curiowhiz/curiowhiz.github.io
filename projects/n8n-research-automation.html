N8n User Research Automation

Reading App Store and Play Store reviews by hand, every day, to catch what users are actually saying is the kind of task that gets skipped the moment a sprint gets busy - which is exactly when it matters most. This n8n workflow runs that research automatically, every day, so feedback themes surface on their own instead of depending on someone remembering to go look.

The Problem

User research on live app reviews is high-signal but low-priority in the moment - it never blocks a release, so it's the first thing to get deprioritized under deadline pressure. The cost shows up later, as a pattern of complaints that everyone noticed individually but no one aggregated in time to act on.

The Approach

An n8n workflow runs on a daily schedule, pulling fresh App Store and Play Store reviews, normalizing them into a common structure, and aggregating them so recurring themes - not just individual complaints - are what surfaces. The workflow does the collection and aggregation; a person still does the judgment call on what to act on.

Who It's For

Product managers and growth teams who need a daily pulse on how users are actually reacting to the live app, without spending an hour a day scrolling through store reviews to find it. It replaces a manual research ritual, not the human judgment that follows it.

Why No Public Demo

This workflow was built for a specific internal review pipeline and isn't set up as a standalone public tool - so there's no live link or open-source repo attached to it, just this write-up of what it does and how it's built.
