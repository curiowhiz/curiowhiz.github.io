Kwalifier

Bank offer pages list every product's terms in the abstract, but almost never answer the one question a customer actually has: "what do I qualify for, right now, given my own account?" Kwalifier answers that directly: a deterministic eligibility verdict backed by an AI-written explanation, grounded strictly in the customer's account data and the bank's own offer catalogue.

The Problem

Eligibility for a bank offer usually depends on account details a customer would have to dig up and cross-reference against fine print themselves: minimum balance, product tenure, transaction history. Most people give up before finishing that comparison, and end up missing benefits they already qualify for.

The Approach

A deterministic rules engine decides eligibility first (eligible, not eligible, or a near-miss) by evaluating the customer's actual account data against the bank's offer catalogue. The verdict itself is never left to the model; an LLM (Google Gemini) is called only afterward, to turn that verdict into a clear, plain-language explanation.

Grounded, Not Guessed

The core guarantee the product is built around: the assistant never speculates about eligibility. Every explanation is grounded in retrieval over the customer's real account data and the bank's official offer terms; if the data doesn't support a claim, the assistant doesn't make it.

The Experience

An eligibility snapshot shows verdicts across the offer catalogue at a glance, and a chat interface lets the customer ask follow-up questions in plain language ("why don't I qualify for the premium card?") and get an answer grounded in their own data rather than a generic FAQ response.

Want to dive deeper?

  • Eligibility is computed, not generated: the rules engine evaluates account data against offer criteria deterministically, so the same inputs always produce the same verdict
  • The LLM's job starts after the verdict exists: Gemini explains a decision that has already been made; it never decides eligibility itself
  • Near-misses are a first-class outcome: customers who are close to qualifying see exactly what's missing, not just a flat no
  • Frontend: TanStack Start on React and TypeScript, deployed on Vercel
  • Reasoning layer: a deterministic rules engine for eligibility verdicts, separate from the AI explanation layer
  • Explanations: Google Gemini, called directly, grounded in retrieved account data and offer terms
  • Demo profiles: multiple sample customer profiles let anyone reviewing the product see different eligibility outcomes side by side

Try It Yourself

Switch between three sample customer profiles and ask what they qualify for, with no account or real data required. Watch the eligibility snapshot update instantly, then ask a follow-up question in chat to see the same verdicts explained in plain language.
